About Smart Tourism

Our Mission

Smart Tourism is dedicated to helping travelers plan their perfect trip with intelligent itineraries and real, honest reviews from the community. We believe that informed travelers make the best journeys.

Our platform combines advanced algorithms with human experiences to create personalized travel plans that maximize your time and help you discover hidden gems along the way.

Features
  • Smart Itineraries - AI-powered trip planning
  • Community Reviews - Real feedback from travelers
  • Live Weather - Current conditions for your destination
  • Star Ratings - Easy-to-read ratings from the community
  • Advanced Search - Find your perfect destination
  • User Accounts - Save your reviews and trips
Technology Stack

Built with modern, scalable technologies:

  • Backend: Flask (Python)
  • Database: SQLAlchemy ORM with SQLite/PostgreSQL
  • Frontend: Bootstrap 5, HTML5, JavaScript
  • APIs: OpenWeatherMap API for real-time weather
