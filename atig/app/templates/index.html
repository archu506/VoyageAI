About ATIG

What is ATIG?

Adaptive Tourism Intelligence Grid is a city-level tourism system that uses machine learning and optimization algorithms to create sustainable, crowd-aware travel experiences.

How It Works

ATIG analyzes historical visitor data, predicts crowd patterns, optimizes routes, and calculates environmental impact to help tourists make informed decisions.

Why It Matters

Unmanaged tourism creates congestion and environmental strain. ATIG distributes visitors intelligently, protecting heritage sites while enhancing experiences.
